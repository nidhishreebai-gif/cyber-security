Evaluation notes

Read metrics in context

  • Precision: lower precision means more normal records may be flagged.
  • Recall: lower recall means some suspicious examples may be missed.
  • Class imbalance: a high accuracy can hide poor detection of a rare class; compare precision, recall, F1, and the confusion matrix.
  • Dataset limits: results depend on label quality, feature selection, and how representative the dataset is. They do not establish real-world detection performance.
